Reading the frames
------------------
The two frames that matter are the top one and the last two.

  `#injectIntoIsolatedWorld` is Firefox building the isolated world (the sandbox) for
  the content script. So the throw happens during content-script *initialisation*.

  `import_lodash` at the top is lodash's `_root.js` module body executing. `_root.js`
  ends with

      var root = freeGlobal || freeSelf || Function('return this')();

  and it is the third operand that the CSP refuses. Because this runs while the module
  graph is initialising, it happens before WXT's entry wrapper reaches `main()`
  (`anime1 ... anime1.js:17` is that wrapper, and the `try/catch` inside it is never
  reached), so the whole content script is dead — not degraded, not partial: no UI at
  all on Firefox.

  `anime1.js:22360:41` is column 41 of the minified line, i.e. the exact `Function(`
  call. In the v1.1.0 bundle the `Function(` token sits at byte offset 270736; the
  minimal repro in repro/ shows the same call at byte 5747 of its own bundle
  (see bundler-diff/new-wxt.js), and not reached at all in the older-wxt build.

Not a Firefox bug
-----------------
MV3 content scripts share the extension CSP, which is `script-src 'self'`.
'unsafe-eval' cannot be used in MV3 `extension_pages` at all — AMO would reject the
submission — so "just allow eval" is not a way out. The bundle has to stop reaching
`eval`/`Function` at module-init time.
